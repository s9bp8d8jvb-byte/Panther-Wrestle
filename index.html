01 • THE MISSION

Buy a piece of Panther history. Help fund the road ahead.

The Panther Legacy Auction is a proposed fundraising event where fans can purchase or bid on Panther wrestling memorabilia, season-ticket packages, and old or new team-issued gear.

The purpose is simple: turn Panther pride into practical support for the wrestling program. Proposed proceeds will help with transportation, lodging, and meals when the team travels to competitions.

06A • FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

Who can participate?

The proposed event is designed for youth wrestlers, families, fans, alumni, and community supporters.

Where does the money go?

For this classroom project, 85% of donations are proposed to support the wrestling program's travel-related costs, including transportation, lodging, and meals.

Is this an official UNI event?

No. This is a student project concept. Official approval would be required before using it as a real fundraiser.

When does registration close?

The proposed deadline is October 10, 2026, or when the 250-person capacity is reached.
